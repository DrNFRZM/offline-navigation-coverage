# Failure-aware arrival times (post hoc secondary analysis)

Added 2026-11-22 after an independent review. **Not part of the preregistered protocol**; it
re-reads the saved per-episode records of the 100 main runs and changes no original result.
Generated by `scripts/failure_aware_time.py`; input digest (SHA-256 over the 100 per-episode files,
LF-normalised) `82a86e4512f8319b033e5964d4f7e793aa933c56bbbea33cbd250bf0577b6bb6`.

Why: the reported first-success times (`first_success_mean_successes_*` in `tables/`) average over
successful episodes only. IQL fails more often than BC in the medium maze, so the success-only means
compare the two methods on different, self-selected subsets of episodes.

Definitions (per policy, over its evaluation episodes; then mean over the five seeds):

- *successes only*: mean first-success step over successful episodes (the earlier metric);
- *failures = H*: failures count as the full horizon H (300 / 600 steps). This is the restricted
  mean time to goal up to H, a lower bound on the mean time had episodes continued;
- *censored median*: median first-success step with failures as +infinity (undefined when at least
  half of the episodes fail; never the case here);
- arrival curves (`fig6_arrival_curves`): fraction of episodes at the goal within t steps.

Statistics as in the main analysis: paired by seed, percentile bootstrap over the five paired
differences (seed-to-seed variability on one recording only), sign counts; no multiplicity
correction. Read descriptively.

## Reading

- Medium maze, IQL - BC on all pairs: -80.0 to -67.2 steps with failures counted as H (all five seeds negative in every condition), against -110.2 to -103.2 steps on successful episodes only and -99.9 to -89.0 steps in the censored median. IQL stays faster after failures are counted, by 65-73% of the success-only gap. Because failed episodes are capped at H, these failure-aware gaps are upper bounds on IQL's advantage in mean time to goal (IQL fails more often); if failed episodes never reach the goal, an uncapped mean is undefined.
- U-maze: pair failures are rare (5 of 10500 pair episodes over all 50 policies), so success-only and failure-aware means differ by at most 1.1 steps per policy; IQL - BC with failures counted is -17.7 to -15.5 steps, with 25 of 25 paired seeds negative.
- Medium maze, full data: the mean arrival curves cross once; IQL's is above BC's until step 408 and below it afterwards. At step 200 (H/3) IQL has reached the goal in 0.865 of pair episodes and BC in 0.504; at step 600 IQL in 0.923 and BC in 0.995 (fig6_arrival_curves).
- Medium maze, B - C (2%), BC, through routes, failures = H: +7.0 steps [+5.0, +9.9], seeds -/0/+ 0/0/5 (successes only: +7.5).
- Medium maze, B - C (2%), IQL, through routes, failures = H: +10.8 steps [-6.8, +28.3], seeds -/0/+ 2/0/3 (successes only: +13.3).
- Medium maze, B2 - C2 (0.2%), BC, through routes, failures = H: +19.7 steps [+16.5, +23.7], seeds -/0/+ 0/0/5 (successes only: +16.0).
- Medium maze, B2 - C2 (0.2%), IQL, through routes, failures = H: +9.7 steps [+0.1, +16.1], seeds -/0/+ 1/0/4 (successes only: +12.3).
